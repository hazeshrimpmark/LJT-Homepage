    Junteng Liu

    About

    Junteng Liu is a first-year PhD candidate at the HKUST NLP Group. Junteng’s research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning. Research interests include:

    • LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning
    • Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)
    • LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability
